Jackpoll is an open-source tool to build, share and analyze surveys and quizzes: a privacy-friendly, self-hostable alternative to closed survey platforms.

Create surveys with a drag-and-drop builder, run scored quizzes (also live, with a leaderboard), collect responses and see results update in real time. Everything is open source, and you can run your own instance.

Features:
• Drag-and-drop builder with pages and conditional logic
• Many question types: short and long answers, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdown, grids, rating, slider, ranking, date, file upload, signature and word cloud
• Text and image blocks between questions for source texts and instructions
• Quiz mode with automatic scoring, plus grading of open answers student by student
• Live quizzes and word clouds with a presentation mode
• Real-time results with charts, PDF and Excel export
• Collaboration with role-based permissions
• Share links and embeddable surveys

Privacy and openness:
• 100% open source (MIT), no ads, no tracking
• Self-hostable: connect the app to your own Jackpoll instance
• No Google Play Services in this build

Notifications:
Push notifications use UnifiedPush. Install a distributor such as ntfy (available on F-Droid) and Jackpoll delivers notifications through it, via ntfy.sh or your own ntfy server.

The app is a client for a Jackpoll server. Use the official instance or point it at your own deployment.
