Jackpoll is an open-source tool to build, share and analyze surveys and quizzes — a privacy-friendly, self-hostable alternative to closed survey platforms.

Create surveys with a drag-and-drop builder, run scored quizzes, collect responses and view results with live charts. Everything is open source, and you can run your own instance.

Features:
• Drag-and-drop survey builder with pages and question logic
• Multiple question types: short answer, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdown, rating grid, ranking
• Quiz mode with correct answers, scoring and automatic grading
• Real-time results with charts, plus CSV export
• Collaboration: invite editors, role-based permissions
• Public share links and embeddable surveys
• Push notifications via UnifiedPush — no Google dependency in the F-Droid build

Privacy & openness:
• 100% open source (MIT)
• Self-hostable: connect the app to your own Jackpoll instance
• The F-Droid build contains no Google Play Services and no proprietary tracking; push works through any UnifiedPush distributor (e.g. ntfy, NextPush)
• No ads

The app is a client for a Jackpoll server. Use the official instance or point it at your own self-hosted deployment.
